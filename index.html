Reliability Snapshot

Two cases, reduced to the moment an assumption was tested

Full write-ups live on the Academic Research and Internships pages. This is the short version of each.

Task

Validate whether conditional sampling improves LLM test-time performance beyond independent sampling.

Assumption flagged

A greedy approximation of the conditional strategy was expected to preserve the same guarantees.

Validated

It did not, under an exact synthetic evaluator. The claim was excluded from the reported results rather than folded in.

Task

Build an AI grading tool to match human graders on open-ended pharmacy course assignments.

Assumption flagged

One general grading prompt was expected to generalize across all seven assignment types.

Validated

It did not. Agreement against human grading was checked every round and item-level instructions were added until it reached 91.2% (κ = 0.81).
